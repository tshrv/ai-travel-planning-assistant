#  A Self-Guided Food Trail in Singapore

From street food to heritage dining haunts and Michelin-starred restaurants, embark on this food trail that will take your taste buds on a wild ride.

##  Day 1

Big eats in Chinatown.

left navigationright navigation

[ ](https://chinatown.sg/dine/chinatown-complex-food-centre/)

####  Chinatown Complex Food Centre

Where better to kick off your foodie adventure than the largest hawker centre in Singapore? You'll be spoilt for choice, from sweet to savoury, and local to international cuisines!

[ ](https://www.facebook.com/tongheng.sg/)

####  Tong Heng Traditional Cantonese Pastries

Savour on toothsome pastries from Tong Heng, which include candied melon pastries, savoury buns and their signature egg tarts.

[ ](https://www.restaurantzen.com/)

####  Zén

Bjorn Frantzén's three Michelin-starred restaurant is famed for its masterful take on Scandinavian and Japanese fusion cuisine.  


[ ](https://chinatown.sg/article/bars-in-club-street-ann-siang-hill/)

####  Ann Siang Hill

Enter this buzzing street, home to exciting bars and restaurants. There’re loads of options to pick from to sate your hunger and slake your thirst.

##  Day 2

Bites and booze around Kampong Gelam.

left navigationright navigation

[ ](https://hjmaimunah.com/)

####  Hjh Maimunah Nasi Padang

Be like a local and start your day with a mountain of rice. The _nasi padang_  (rice with a medley of Malay dishes) served here is one of the best on the island.

[ ](https://visitkamponggelam.org/haji-lane-boutiques/)

####  Haji Lane

Make your way to this trendy lane for some great live music. Come nightfall, the many bars and cafés in the area set up al fresco seating areas as live musicians belt out tunes on the narrow lane.

[ ](https://www.facebook.com/BlancoCourtPrawnMee/)

####  Blanco Court Prawn Mee

A sinful Singaporean dish, dig into a rich prawn noodle broth served with al dente noodles and juicy prawns.

[ ](https://birdsofparadise.sg/)

####  Birds of Paradise

Delight in refreshing and smooth gelatos with botanical inspired flavours of nature using regional herbs like basil and blue ginger.

[ ](https://atlasbar.sg/)

####  Atlas

Grab refreshing cocktails at this bar which has Gotham-like exterior and luxurious 1920s inspired interior. It is decked with intricate art and a Gin Tower with over 1,000 bottles on display.

##  Day 3

Culture meets flavour around the island.

left navigationright navigation

[ ](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Tekka%2BCentre/@1.3060054,103.8271133,15z/data=!3m1!5s0x31da17bbf357d8d1:0x78a106db2d7933a7!4m6!3m5!1s0x31da19f1dd1e4843:0xe759a7414a9bcedc!8m2!3d1.3063839!4d103.8507014!16s%252Fm%252F0261l5y?coh=210790&entry=tts&g_ep=EgoyMDI0MDUyOC4wKgBIAVAD)

####  Tekka Centre

Let the fragrance of spices and curries pique your appetite in the morning. The hawker centre specialises in food from across the Indian sub-continent, as well as local delights like duck rice and fish soup.

[ ](https://www.facebook.com/ANoodleStory/)

####  A Noodle Story

Taste a bowl of fusion handmade noodles at this Singapore-style ramen stall, a Michelin Bib Gourmand awardee since 2016. Its local and international influences reflect Singapore's rich culinary heritage.

[ ](https://www.comoepicurean.com/restaurants/candlenut/)

####  Candlenut

A one Michelin star restaurant, Candlenut serves up traditional Peranakan fare with a creative twist and an Indonesian touch.

[ ](https://www.mamadiamsg.com/)

####  Mama Diam

A speakeasy bar that infuses elements of our local provision store common in the past. Explore a food and drinks menu that puts a modern twist to iconic local favourites.

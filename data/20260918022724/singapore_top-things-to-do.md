#### [ AltitudeXDefy gravity, soar indoors and feel the adrenaline of flight at AltitudeX on Sentosa Island. ](https://altitudex.com/sg/)

#### [ Forest Bathing by Forest with YouEmbrace nature's healing touch, immerse in lush greenery and rejuvenate amidst the serenity of a forest. ](https://www.forestwithyou.com/)

#### [ Rainforest Wild AdventureGlide through the canopy or swing high into the sky with Adventure+ activities, taking in heart-racing views while spotting animals beneath. Don't miss the exclusive Okapi and Diana Monkey, now in Singapore for the first time, and many more! ](https://www.mandai.com/en/rainforest-wild-adventure.html? cmp=sg|rfw_adventure|ref|vs|par|stb|||things_to_do_unique_experiences&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_unique_experiences)

#### [ Ubin Bisect KayakingGlide through serene mangroves and encounter diverse wildlife, connecting with nature at Pulau Ubin. ](https://www.adventures.network/ubin-bisect-kayaking)

#### [ Bird ParadiseTake flight to Asia's largest bird park, home to more than 3,500 birds from over 400 species. Explore 10 immersive walk-through aviaries, inspired by habitats from around the world. ](https://www.mandai.com/en/bird-paradise.html?cmp=sg%7Cbpd%7Cref%7Cvs%7Cpar%7Cstb%7C%7C%7Cthings_to_do_family_fun&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_family_fun)

#### [ Kulnari Mystery GolfSolve intriguing puzzles, play mini golf and unravel thrilling mysteries in this immersive mystery golf course. ](https://www.kulnari.com/)

#### [ Synthesis Bar Savour mixology magic, tasting eclectic concoctions amidst chic and vibrant surroundings. ](https://www.synthesis.sg/)

#### [ ExploriaExplore one of Southeast Asia's largest indoor, nature-themed multimedia attractions, where rarely seen realms of the world come to life. Use your RFID wristband to unlock interactive touchpoints and discover the wonders of nature — past and present. ](https://www.mandai.com/en/exploria.html? cmp=sg|exploria|ref|vs|par|stb|||things_to_do_unique_experiences&utm_campaign=vs&utm_medium=par&utm_source=stb&utm_content=things_to_do_unique_experiences)

#### [ Naturalist Night Adventure Embark on a thrilling wildlife safari adventure, spotting nocturnal creatures under the moonlit sky at Sentosa Island. ](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/events/sentosa-naturalist-night-adventure/)

#### [ TrifectaExperience the ultimate thrill and unleash your adventurous spirit at Trifecta. Hit the snowboard, ride the waves, and feel the excitement of the skate bowl. ](https://trifectasingapore.com/)

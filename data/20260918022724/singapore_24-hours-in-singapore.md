#  24 Hours In Singapore

Pressed for time? Take in some of Singapore’s best sights in our 24 hour guide.

## Morning

##  

#### [Ya Kun Kaya ToastStart your day with a quintessential Singaporean breakfast. Indulge in a hearty kaya toast (a traditional coconut jam and a slab of butter sandwiched between toast), combined with local coffee to kickstart your adventure. ](https://app.yakun.com/)

#### [ChinatownTake in the sights and sounds of Chinatown, and head to the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Masjid Jamae Mosque and Sri Mariamman Temple - all of which possess striking architecture and fascinating insights, testament to Singapore's multiculturalism. ](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/)

#### [Chinatown Complex Food CentreStop by Singapore's largest hawker centre, home to a myraid of stalls, some even holders of the Michelin Star and Bib Gourmand. Indulge in dishes from Lian He Ben Ji Claypot or Liao Fan Hong Kong Soya Sauce Chicken Rice & Noodle. ](https://chinatown.sg/dine/chinatown-complex-food-centre/)

## Afternoon

##  

#### [Raffles HotelMake your way to the iconic Raffles Hotel, known for its old-world charm and colonial architecture. Grab a Singapore Sling cocktail at the Long bar – widely regarded as the national drink of the country.  
](https://www.raffles.com/singapore/)

#### [Marina Bay SandsNo trip to Singapore is complete without a visit to Marina Bay Sands. Explore floors of luxury retailers or head up to the Sands SkyPark® and be wowed by the 360-degree views on the observation deck.  
](https://www.marinabaysands.com/)

#### [Gardens By The BayCatch a glimpse of the futuristic-looking Supertrees, which is a vertical garden that spans 25-50 metres in height and is a sanctuary for nature lovers. ](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/marina-bay/gardens-by-the-bay/)

## Evening

##  

#### [Lau Pa SatDine under the stars and immerse yourself in the bustling atmosphere while savouring authentic Singapore cuisine and international favourites. We recommend ordering the grilled skewers at Satay Street for an** ** authentic street hawker experience.  
](https://www.laupasat.sg/)

#### [Singapore River Bumboat CruiseHop on a traditional bumboat for a relaxing journey to catch Singapore's landmarks and the bright lights of Singapore's urban night scape, while experiencing an unforgettable ride along the windy Singapore River.  
](https://rivercruise.com.sg/)

#### [Clarke QuayThe night doesn't end yet - buzzing nightlife awaits at Clarke Quay's colourful array of dining and bar offerings. Enjoy live music and drinks at the myraid of bars or hit up the clubs to show off your dance moves.  
](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/singapore-river/)

## Late Night

##  

#### [Mustafa CentreNot much of a party person? Head to Mustafa Centre – a shopping complex that sells just about everything you can think of. Best of all, it’s open till late at night.  
](https://www.instagram.com/mustafacentre/?hl=en)

#### [Zam Zam RestaurantGrab supper at late-night joint Zam Zam to recharge from the day’s activities. We recommend digging into their famous murtabak with a side of curry. ](https://zamzamsingapore.has.restaurant/)

#### [Swee Choon Tim SumAlternatively, flock to this dim sum establishment which consists of bite-sized portions of food served in steamer baskets or small plates. Relish in a range of textures and flavours to end your night.  
](https://www.sweechoon.com/)

#  Enjoy Singapore In 7 Days

With a week to spare, discover how east meets west, nature and urban beauty intertwine, and the past meets present, with our guide to Singapore's most iconic spots.

##  Day 1: Urban Nature, Modern Marvels

Morning

Afternoon

Evening

#### [Marina Bay An iconic district in Singapore, that's guaranteed to bombard your senses, with a mix of nature, arts and shopping, there's something for everyone. ](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/marina-bay/)

#### [Gardens by the BaySpend the day marveling at the flora on display at the Flower Dome, and exploring the mist-filled landscape of the Cloud Forest. ](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/)

#### [Marina Bay Sands & The ShoppesExplore floors of luxury retailers, with the largest collection of coveted designer boutiques in Asia. ](https://www.marinabaysands.com/)

##  Day 2: Thrills and Spills at Sentosa

Morning

Afternoon

Evening

#### [SentosaSun, sand and surf await you on your second day. Located off our city's southern shore, Sentosa island is known for its white beaches and family-friendly activities. ](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/sentosa-island/)

#### [Universal Studios SingaporeExperience the magic of the movies at this theme park, home to a host of exhilarating rides, spread across six movie-themed districts from Hollywood to The Lost World.  
](https://www.rwsentosa.com/en/play/universal-studios-singapore)

#### [Singapore OceanariumAlternatively, embark on an odyssey beneath the waves and find captivating species from the deep, including Atlantic Sea Nettles, Fried Egg Sea Jellies and more. ](https://www.rwsentosa.com/en/play/singapore-oceanarium)

##  Day 3: Culture and Heritage

Morning

Afternoon

Evening

#### [Civic DistrictSingapore’s rich colonial history and bright future intertwine in the Civic District, where museums and colonial buildings sit alongside skyscrapers. ](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/)

#### [National Museum of SingaporeExplore our city's culture and heritage at this grand old dame of a local museum, where history comes to life. ](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/national-museum-of-singapore/)

#### [National Gallery SingaporeHoused in buildings that were once our country's City Hall and Supreme Court, National Gallery Singapore boasts over 8,000 works of art. ](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/civic-district/national-gallery-singapore/)

##  Day 4: Wildlife Awaits

Morning

Evening

#### [MandaiMake your way to Northern Singapore to ignite your love for nature with the majestic animals, nature-themed attractions and lush greenery at Mandai.  
](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/mandai/)

#### [Bird ParadiseHome to over 3,500 birds, be immersed in naturalistic walk-through aviaries that reflect different habitats of the world. ](https://www.mandai.com/en/bird-paradise.html)

](https://www.mandai.com/en/singapore-zoo.html)

Afternoon

Evening

Afternoon

Afternoon

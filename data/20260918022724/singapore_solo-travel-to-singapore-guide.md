##  Wander Freely, Discover Deeply

Planning a Singapore solo trip opens up a world of freedom and discovery. Wander heritage neighbourhoods on your own, take a stroll in tranquil gardens, or enjoy a Michelin-starred meal. Singapore offers an exciting blend of culture, cuisine, and a city in nature that makes every solo journey unforgettable.

##  Young Voyager Victor

Adventure on a budget. Victor seeks out value-for-money gems and authentic encounters that stretch his dollar without compromising on experience.

left navigationright navigation

[ ](https://culturecurious.biz/private-singapore-group-walking-tours/private-singapore-day-tour-2/)

####  Culture, Art and Architecture Private Tour

Delve into Singapore’s colonial past by strolling through Chinatown and the Civic District and hearing stories of our iconic landmarks told through public art. 

[ ](https://tribe-tours.com/product/disappearing-trades/)

####  Disappearing Trades

Take a trip down memory lane by learning about Singapore's fast-disappearing old-school businesses. You will visit a traditional bakery, an ancestral paper money shop and a coffee roasting factory.

[ ](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/trails/Little-India-Heritage-Trail-Walk-of-Faiths)

####  Little India Heritage Trail - Walk of Faiths

Explore Little India at your own pace with this trail. It takes you past temples, mosques, and churches that stand just a few streets apart, reflecting the neighbourhood's rich tapestry of faiths.

[ ](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/telok-blangah-hill-park/special-features/henderson-waves)

####  Henderson Waves

Venture above the tree line and admire the view of the surrounding greenery from the highest pedestrian bridge in Singapore.

[ ](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/central-catchment-nature-reserve)

####  Central Catchment Reserve

Spanning over 2,000 hectares of forest with more than 20km of trails, the Central Catchment Nature Reserve is Singapore's largest nature reserve. Highlights include the Treetop Walk's panoramic views and the calm waters of MacRitchie Reservoir.

[ ](https://www.roots.gov.sg/places/places-landing/trails/my-queenstown-heritage-trail)

####  Queenstown Heritage Trail

Wander past old landmarks and modern redevelopments of Singapore's first satellite town. Uncover stories of how this once-quiet estate became a living record of our city's growth.

[ ](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/SBG)

####  Singapore Botanic Gardens

This UNESCO World Heritage Site is the perfect spot for a picnic, a jog or a reprieve from the buzz of the city.

##  Wanderlust Wendy

Wanderlust Wendy represents the modern female solo traveller. Whether it is a digital detox, a wellness retreat, or a few days of quietly exploring Singapore, she values her wellbeing and safety.

left navigationright navigation

[ ](https://pcn.nparks.gov.sg/our-pcn/c2c/)

####  Coast-to-Coast Trail

Stretching 36km across Singapore, this trail links nature areas, parks, and park connectors from Jurong Lake Gardens to Coney Island. This rewarding trail can also be enjoyed in parts, from a leisurely evening walk to a scenic park connector ride.

[ ](https://www.nowherebaths.com/)

####  Nowhere Baths

A contemporary gender-neutral bathhouse, Nowhere Baths offers hot baths, cold plunges, a sauna, and a steam room. Each experience is designed to soothe muscles, quiet the mind, and promote recovery.

[ ](https://www.teachapter.com/)

####  Tea Chapter

Tea Chapter's appreciation workshops offer solo travellers a meditative journey into Chinese tea culture. As Singapore's largest and oldest teahouse, it invites you to slow down and embrace the calm ritual of brewing and savouring tea in its most authentic form.

[ ](https://www.gentle.sg/)

####  Gentle Walks

Gentle Walks leads you through forest therapy around Singapore's beautiful parks, using mindful invitations and unhurried wandering to help you reconnect with nature and yourself.

[ ](https://railcorridor.nparks.gov.sg/visit-rail-corridor/)

####  Rail Corridor

Once a railway line, this 24km green passage connects major parks and nature reserves, with the old Bukit Timah Railway Station and rustic tracks offering many solo photo stops along the way.

[ ](https://www.sentosa.com.sg/en/things-to-do/events/sentosa-naturalist-night-adventure/)

####  Naturalist Night Adventure

Embark on a thrilling wildlife safari adventure, spotting nocturnal creatures under the moonlit sky at Sentosa Island.

[ ](https://www.artyzen.com/en/hotels/artyzen-singapore/)

####  Artyzen Singapore

A sanctuary in the city, Artyzen offers solo female travellers wellness and workout initiatives designed for solo rejuvenation. Enjoy a morning yoga class, take a swim in the rooftop "floating pool" which features a partial glass bottom, or indulge in gemstone-infused spa rituals.

#  Freedom to Roam, From Day to Night

Consistently ranked among the world's safest cities, Singapore is a top destination for solo travellers seeking peace of mind, whether you're dining out late or venturing into street art alleys. Our public transport network is fast, reliable, and easy to navigate. English is the primary spoken language, making it simple to ask for directions, order meals, or strike up a friendly conversation.

##  Explorer Emma

Explorer Emma is not here to simply sightsee. She is here for adrenaline-fuelled adventures, the buzz of festivals, or mingling with locals in Singapore.

left navigationright navigation

[ ](https://mrbucket.com.sg/collections/experiences)

####  Mr Bucket Chocolaterie

Build your own chocolate bar, discover where chocolate comes from, and learn to identify high-quality cacao beans. You will explore the craft behind bean-to-bar production before taking home your personalised creations.

[ ](https://sg.oola-lab.com/collections/singapore-workshops/products/scents-of-the-spice-route)

####  Oo La Lab

Inspired by the spices that once passed through Singapore's bustling ports, this perfume workshop lets you craft a scent using historical ingredients such as clove, nutmeg, and cinnamon.

[ ](https://www.mandai.com/en/dine-and-shop/dining-outlets/rainforest-wild-adventure/cavern-restaurant.html)

####  Cavern Restaurant

Marvel at the towering rock formations while savouring a feast for the senses at Singapore's first and only restaurant in a cave setting.

[ ](https://www.splatpainthouse.com/)

####  Splat Paint House

Indulge in art therapy and create your very own abstract masterpiece at this workshop, where you will fling rainbow-hued paint at blank canvases.

[ ](https://appetitesg.com/)

####  Appetite

This gem is equal parts restaurant, art gallery, and vinyl lounge. With its art-filled walls, warm lighting, and the soft crackle of records in the background, this intimate space invites guests to fully embrace the moment.

[ ](https://trifectasingapore.com/)

####  Trifecta

Whether you are looking to surf, skate or snowboard, you will be able to amp up the adrenaline at this sports facility, the first in Asia to combine all three experiences.

[ ](https://www.mandai.com/en/stay/glamping-in-the-wild/reservoir-retreat.html)

####  Glamping in the Wild

Choose between two signature all-inclusive glamping stays – Reservoir Retreat or Crocodile Lodge, complete with park admissions, activities, meals, and guided tours at the Mandai Wildlife Reserve.

##  Bleisure Brandon

Brandon works hard and unwinds even harder on business trips, indulging in trendy restaurants and connecting with new people in vibrant social spaces.

left navigationright navigation

[ ](https://taogroup.com/venues/marquee-singapore/)

####  Marquee

Dance under the dazzling lights at Singapore's ultimate nightlife destination. Globally celebrated DJs set the stage, while a giant Ferris wheel fitted with photobooths and a three-storey spiral slide make the party as playful as it is unforgettable.

[ ](https://hideaway.sg/)

####  Hideaway

Unwind with soothing massages, rejuvenating saunas, and refreshing cold plunges in this serene hideaway. After a day of retail therapy in New Bahru, it's the perfect place for a well-deserved solo pampering session.

[ ](https://brassliondistillery.com/pages/gin-school)

####  Brass Lion Brewery

Select from aromatic botanicals, distil your blend in a copper still, and bottle a uniquely personal gin at Singapore's first full-fledged micro-distillery. This hands-on workshop offers not just a creative solo activity but also a chance to mingle with fellow gin lovers.

[ ](https://minmed.sg/fitness/)

####  Jewel Changi

Work up a sweat and burn some calories amid the beauty of Jewel Changi's indoor greenery with Minmed's exhilarating fitness classes.

[ ](https://www.rwsentosa.com/en/dine/signature-restaurants/ocean-restaurant)

####  Ocean Restaurant

The deep blue sea's splendour awaits you at Ocean Restaurant. The floor-to-ceiling glass panels will give you an unforgettable view of the restaurant's underwater habitat, filled with sharks and manta rays.

[ ](https://www.willowrestaurant.sg/)

####  Willow

MICHELIN Star

Tucked within a shophouse in Singapore's bustling core, Willow has earned a Michelin star for its elegantly composed cuisine. Its daily-changing tasting menus spotlight seasonal ingredients with a sustainable touch.

[ ](https://www.raffles.com/singapore/dining/long-bar/)

####  Long Bar

Home to the iconic Singapore Sling cocktail, this legendary bar needs little introduction. With a menu of classic and sophisticated cocktails, the bar's rich and earthy decor exudes elegance.

####  Mass Rapid Transit Map

For convenient navigation throughout the city, you may download the [MyTransport App](https://apps.apple.com/sg/app/mytransport-sg/id1306661188 "Follow link") for public transport routes and timings. You may also download the Singapore MRT map, which offers a comprehensive overview of the MRT system.

[Get a MRT map](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/public_transport/rail_network.html)

####  Language

You can communicate in English with Singaporeans, who are mostly fluent in it. Many also speak Mandarin Chinese, Malay or Tamil. You may also encounter Singlish, a unique colloquial form of English that blends with various languages and dialects spoken in Singapore.

[Learn how to speak Singlish](https://www.visitsingapore.com/travel-tips/about-singapore/#we-speak-singlish)
